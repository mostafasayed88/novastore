# B2B and Wholesale Program

## Who qualifies

Businesses, schools, and government offices ordering 10 units or more of the same product qualify for NovaStore's B2B program. Retail customers who want fewer than 10 units order through the normal store checkout.

## Discount tiers

Orders of 10-24 units receive a 5% discount. Orders of 25-49 units receive an 8% discount. Orders of 50 units or more receive a custom quote prepared by our sales team, often with free delivery and extended payment terms.

## How to get a quote

Tell the assistant your company name, contact details, the product you need, and the quantity. This creates a sales lead, and our B2B team sends a formal quotation within one business day. Quotes are valid for 7 days.

## Payment terms

B2B orders are paid by bank transfer against a proforma invoice. Approved companies can request net-14 payment terms after their first completed order. All prices exclude VAT; VAT is added on the final invoice.

## Business account benefits

A NovaStore business account includes: a dedicated account manager, priority support with faster ticket response, consolidated monthly invoicing, and access to the extended warranty addendum for accidental damage.

## Delivery for B2B orders

Bulk orders ship within 3-5 business days of payment confirmation. Large orders are delivered with a scheduled appointment so your IT team can receive and verify the units.

## Returns for B2B orders

Unopened units can be returned within 14 days of delivery, same as retail. Defective units are replaced under warranty without counting against the return window.
