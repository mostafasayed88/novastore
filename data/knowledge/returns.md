# Returns Policy

## Return window

You can return any product within 14 days of delivery. The product must be unopened, unused, and in its original packaging with all accessories and the invoice.

## Opened electronics

Opened electronics can only be returned if they are defective. A device that works correctly but was simply opened or used is not eligible for return after the seal is broken.

## Headphones and earbuds

In-ear earbuds cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is opened, unless they are defective. Over-ear headphones (like the Sony WH-1000XM5) can be returned within the 14-day window if the packaging is intact and unopened.

## Damaged on arrival

If a product arrives damaged or defective, report it within 48 hours of delivery through chat or a support ticket. We ship a free replacement after the carrier picks up the damaged item - no extra cost to you.

## How to start a return

Contact our assistant with your order number and the reason for the return. Our team confirms eligibility within one business day, then sends a courier to pick up the item. Keep the product in its original box until pickup.

## Refunds after a return

After the returned item passes inspection, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3-7 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer; our team will ask for your account details.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards and software licenses are non-returnable. Clearance items marked "final sale" on the product page are also non-returnable.
