# Warranty Policy

## Standard warranty

Every NovaStore product includes a 12-month manufacturer warranty against factory defects, starting from the delivery date.

## Extended warranty on laptops and monitors

Dell laptops and LG monitors include a 24-month warranty. Lenovo ThinkPad business laptops include a 36-month warranty. The exact warranty length for each product is listed on its product page.

## What the warranty covers

Manufacturing defects: hardware failure under normal use, dead pixels on screens, battery failures, and faulty ports or buttons. The warranty does NOT cover accidental damage (drops, cracks), liquid damage, power surges, or unauthorized repairs.

## How to claim warranty

Contact our assistant or open a support ticket with your order number and a short description of the defect. For laptops, also include the serial number. Our team schedules a diagnosis, usually within 2 business days.

## Repairs and replacement

If the defect is confirmed and the product is within warranty, we repair it or replace it with an equivalent model. If the exact model is out of stock, we offer the current equivalent or a refund of the item's value.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

For products outside the warranty window, we offer paid diagnosis and repair. You receive a written quote before any work starts, and you decide whether to proceed.

## Business customers

Companies with a NovaStore business account can add an extended warranty plan covering accidental damage. Ask our sales team for the business warranty addendum when requesting a quote.
