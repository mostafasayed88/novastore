# Shipping Policy

## Delivery zones and timelines

NovaStore ships across Egypt. Delivery takes 1-2 business days in Cairo and Giza, 2-3 business days in Alexandria and the Delta, and 3-5 business days in Upper Egypt and remote areas. All timelines are counted in business days (Sunday to Thursday); orders placed on Friday or Saturday are dispatched on Sunday.

## Shipping fees

Shipping costs a flat 50 EGP per order. Orders with a total of 3,000 EGP or more ship free of charge. Cash-on-delivery orders carry no extra fee.

## Carriers and tracking

We ship with Bosta, Aramex, and Mylerz. As soon as your order leaves our warehouse you receive an SMS with a tracking code. You can also ask our assistant for the latest status of your order at any time - just give us your order number.

## Delays

Public holidays, bad weather, and address problems can delay delivery by 1-2 business days. If your order is more than 2 business days past its estimated date, contact us and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

## International shipping

We do not ship outside Egypt yet. International shipping is planned for a future phase; follow our announcements page for updates.

## Changing the delivery address

The address can be changed only while an order is in "processing" status. Once an order is "shipped", the carrier must complete delivery to the original address.
