# NovaStore FAQ

## Payment methods

We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), cash on delivery, and bank transfer for business orders. Instalment plans with partner banks are available for orders above 10,000 EGP.

## How do I track my order?

Give the assistant your order number (for example: "Where is order 5482?") and it will return the live status, the carrier, and the estimated delivery date. You also receive SMS updates at every stage.

## Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled while they are in "pending_payment" or "processing" status. Once an order is "shipped" it can no longer be cancelled - you can refuse delivery instead, and the item returns to us for a standard return refund.

## Out-of-stock items

When a product is out of stock it usually restocks within 2-3 weeks. The assistant can tell you the current stock status of any product; you can also ask us to notify you when it returns.

## Invoices

Electronic invoices are emailed automatically after delivery. Business customers can request invoices with their company tax details; contact support with your order number and company information.

## Store and support hours

Our online store operates 24/7. The AI assistant answers immediately, any time. Human support is available Sunday to Thursday, 9:00-18:00 Cairo time.

## Price matching

We do not match competitors' prices. Prices in EGP include VAT unless stated otherwise on the product page.

## Talking to a human

You can ask the assistant for "a human" or "an agent" at any time. It opens a support ticket immediately and a person follows up by email or phone - the assistant will not keep arguing with you.
